### dynamic range
Dynamic Range (DR)
given Emin - the minimum detectable irradiation 
given Emax - the saturation irradiation 

the DR of the sensor is defined as Emax/Emin
like the signal to noise ratio it is often expressed in bits or dbs

As it is the case of the SNR, also for the DR the higher is the better. Indeed, the higher the DR the better is the ability of the
sensor to simultaneously capture in one image both the dark and bright structures of the scene.
An active research field in image processing deals with creating High Dynamic Range (HDR) images by combining together a
sequence of images of the same subject taken under different exposure times

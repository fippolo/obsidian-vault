### panorama stitching 
 creating a larger image  by aligning two images of the same scene
 - The two images may be aligned by estimating a homography, which requires at least 4 correspondences (more is better)
 - Find “salient points” independently in the two images
 - Compute a local “description” for each salient point
 - Compare descriptions to find matching pairs
 - once you have 4 matches you can stitch the image together

## Local Invariant feature
being able to map two points of two different images if they are the projection of the same 2d point.
several computer vision tasks deal with finding a "Cor"
